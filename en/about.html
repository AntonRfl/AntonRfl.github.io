About

Welcome to my website! I am a Software Engineer and Tech Enthusiast.

My work and interests lie at the intersection of Software Architecture, IT Security, and Data Science. I am passionate about building clean, maintainable systems and exploring the underlying mathematics and security mechanisms that make modern technology work.

Prior to starting this blog, I have spent my time diving deep into various technical fields—from mastering Aspect-Oriented Programming to analyzing data sources with Python. I am driven by the challenge of solving complex problems and the continuous process of learning.

I enjoy exploring a wide range of topics and sharing what I find, which is exactly what this blog is for! Thank you for reading.
